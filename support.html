Common questions

How many skeins do I need? The colour key assumes two strands on the fabric count you chose. One skein covers about 1,770 full stitches on 14 count.

How do I print? Export the PDF and open it with your printer app or a print service from the share sheet. Pages overlap by two rows so you can line them up.

I bought Pro on another phone. Open Settings > Pro > Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google account.

How do I move my projects? Library > Back up all projects, or export a single project file, then import it on the new phone.

Can I turn off personalised ads? Settings > Privacy options (shown where consent applies).
