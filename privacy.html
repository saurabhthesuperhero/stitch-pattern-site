Needlenook privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Needlenook: Cross Stitch Maker ("the app") is made by Saikaizen Innovations ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

Your photos and patterns

Photos you choose are processed on your device to create patterns. Photos, patterns, progress and project files are stored only on your device. We never upload them, and we have no server that receives them. When you share or export a file, it goes only where you send it through your phone's share sheet. We do not ask for camera, microphone, location or contacts access; photos are picked with the system photo picker.

No account

The app has no sign-in and does not ask for your name or email.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use Meta Audience Network and Liftoff (Vungle) as mediation partners. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and personalised ads where you allow it. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK and some US states) we ask for your choice with Google's consent tool before any ad request; you can change it any time under Settings > Privacy options. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used (for example "a pattern was created" or "a PDF was exported", with grid size and colour count) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (device model, OS version, app version and the technical details of the crash). These do not include your photos or patterns. Data is processed by Google under its terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules at most one local reminder a day on your device. No data leaves your phone for this.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Everything you create lives on your device; deleting a project or uninstalling the app removes it. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for up to 14 months. You can ask us to delete any analytics data linked to your device by emailing us.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data.
